Our Story

Letiza Bakery began in a small home kitchen in Mokopane, where the smell of fresh bread drew neighbours to the door long before there was ever a shop sign. What started as a few loaves and cakes baked for family and friends soon grew into something bigger — a bakery built on the same care and attention that went into every batch from day one.

Every recipe carries a simple promise: nothing leaves our oven unless it's exactly right. Our founder has a saying that guides everything we bake —

"Expect no disappointments."

It's more than a phrase — it's the standard behind every loaf, cake, and pastry we make. Whether you're picking up your weekly bread or collecting a cake for your child's birthday, you can trust that it was made with the same care as if it were for our own family.

Our Mission

To provide the Mokopane community with high-quality, freshly baked goods made with care — and never settle for anything less than the best.

Our Vision

To become the neighbourhood's most trusted bakery, known for consistency, warmth, and delicious baking that never disappoints.

What We Stand For

  • Freshness first: Everything is baked daily, never sitting on a shelf.
  • No shortcuts: Quality ingredients and honest recipes, every time.
  • Community at heart: We're proud to be part of Mokopane, one loaf at a time.
